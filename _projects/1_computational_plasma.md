My doctoral research used global magnetohydrodynamic simulation coupled with kinetic models to study transport and energy conversion in large-scale plasma systems. The work involved numerical model coupling, validation, and quantitative analysis of simulation output.

Within this area I work on the following projects:

- **Charged-particle transport.** Numerical studies of how source regions and boundary conditions affect transport in coupled physical systems.
- **Reverse test-particle tracing.** Particle-tracing and model-comparison methods used to identify source regions and their contribution to the modeled distribution.
