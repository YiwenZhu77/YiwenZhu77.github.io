Long historical records and simulation outputs can support forecasting workflows for complex physical systems. My work in this area emphasizes data quality, feature design, validation splits, error analysis, and reproducible comparison of different prediction methods.

Within this area I work on the following projects:

- **Statistical forecasting from long observation records.** Gradient-boosted models trained on multi-decade data, with consistent evaluation splits and error analysis.
- **Multi-horizon forecasting.** Comparison of numerical and data-driven approaches for predicting time-series quantities at several forecast horizons.
