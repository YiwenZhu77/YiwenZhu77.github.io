**Zhu AI & Scientific Computing LLC** is the consulting practice of Yiwen Zhu, Ph.D. (Physics, Rice University; M.C.S., Rice University). It is a sole proprietorship registered in Harris County, Texas.

We provide research, development, consulting and education services in science, engineering and technology.

## Services

1. Modeling, simulation and numerical analysis of physical and engineering systems
2. Data analysis, statistics, analytics and visualization
3. Scientific software engineering in Python, C++, and Fortran
4. High-performance and parallel computing using MPI and OpenMP
5. Quantitative modeling and decision support
6. R&D support, technical due diligence and literature review
7. Technical writing and documentation
8. Training and tutoring in physics, mathematics, and programming

## How to engage

Projects are billed hourly or at a fixed price under a short written service agreement. Each project starts with a free scoping call and a written quote. Invoices are issued in USD; payment is accepted by bank transfer, card, Alipay or WeChat Pay.

**Cancellation and refunds.** Either party may end a project with 7 days written notice; the client pays only for work completed up to that point. Prepaid amounts for work not yet performed are refunded in full within 14 days.
