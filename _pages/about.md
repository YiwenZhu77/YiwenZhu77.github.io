I am a final-year PhD candidate in space physics at [Rice University](https://physics.rice.edu/), advised by [Frank Toffoletto](https://profiles.rice.edu/faculty/frank-r-toffoletto). I am concurrently completing an MS in Computer Science (Data &amp; AI) at Rice, and earned my BS at the [Southern University of Science and Technology](https://www.sustech.edu.cn/) (SUSTech). My research studies the dynamics and transport of charged particles throughout Earth's magnetosphere, using global magnetohydrodynamic simulation and kinetic test-particle modeling. I also work on operational space-weather forecasting and on applying machine learning to space-physics questions.

My main interest right now is **AI infrastructure and machine learning systems**: how large models are trained across many GPUs, how those GPUs communicate and get scheduled, how computation is mapped onto a cluster, and the systems behind serving large language model and multi agent applications. This grows out of my background in high performance and parallel computing (MPI, OpenMP, CUDA, Rust, C++), where I schedule and map large simulation workloads onto thousands of cores, and out of my recent first author survey of large language model multi agent systems and the protocols their agents use to communicate ([published in *Future Internet*](https://www.mdpi.com/1999-5903/18/6/326)). I am also interested in the reliability of large language models on long technical tasks.

I am on the academic and industry job market this cycle, and I am looking for a fall research assistantship in machine learning systems and AI infrastructure. Please feel free to reach out at <a href="mailto:yz167@rice.edu">yz167@rice.edu</a>.
