I earned my PhD in Physics and Master of Computer Science (MCS) at [Rice University](https://physics.rice.edu/) on August 31, 2026. My doctoral advisor was [Frank Toffoletto](https://profiles.rice.edu/faculty/frank-r-toffoletto), and I earned my BS at the [Southern University of Science and Technology](https://www.sustech.edu.cn/) (SUSTech). I now own Zhu AI &amp; Scientific Computing LLC, an early-stage scientific computing business focused on internal research and development and customer discovery. My doctoral training was in computational physics and numerical methods, with research on the simulation and analysis of complex physical systems. I also work on scientific software and reproducible computational workflows.

My current focus is **scientific computing and numerical modeling**: high-performance simulation, reproducible scientific workflows, computational methods for physical systems, and the tools used to analyze and communicate model results. This builds on my doctoral training in computational physics and my master's work in computer science.

I am open to research and industry opportunities in scientific computing, numerical modeling, and scientific software engineering. Please feel free to reach out at <a href="mailto:yz167@rice.edu">yz167@rice.edu</a>.
